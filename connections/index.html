Pepdevs Connections

Pepdevs Connections is a private, self-hosted service that lets approved personal applications use accounts their owners explicitly connect.

Why it accesses Gmail

Connections reads selected email messages for two workflows: retrieving retailer sign-in verification codes for Household receipt collection, and finding requested usage-report notifications for a private reporting application. An approved application may also retrieve attachments when its grant explicitly permits them.

Your authorization

You sign in and approve access on Google's pages. Connections requests the Gmail read-only scope, which allows reading messages and attachments across the authorized mailbox. Its application grants further restrict which inboxes, senders, subjects and operations each approved application can use.

Connections cannot send, modify or delete Gmail messages. Google authorization tokens stay on private infrastructure. Applications receive their own restricted service credentials.

Private operation

The service is available only on the operator's private network. This public website contains information about the service and provides no mailbox access or sign-up.
