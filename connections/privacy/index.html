Pepdevs Connections privacy notice

Effective 7 October 2026

Scope and purpose

Pepdevs Connections is operated privately for account owners and applications approved by its operator. It reads authorized Gmail accounts to support retailer sign-in verification, requested report discovery and explicitly permitted attachment retrieval.

Google data accessed

The Gmail read-only authorization can access mailbox messages and attachments. Connections processes the account's email address, Google authorization tokens, message identifiers, timestamps, sender and recipient headers, subjects, message bodies and requested attachment bytes. Each application has a separate grant restricting the accounts and operations it may use, with sender and subject filters and additional attachment limits where applicable.

Use and recipients

Data is returned only to approved applications for their configured workflows. Household uses verification codes to authenticate to the retailer selected by the account owner. A reporting application uses requested report notifications and permitted files for its import workflow. Google processes the authorization and Gmail API requests. The service does not sell data or use Google data for advertising, credit decisions or training generalized artificial-intelligence models.

Pepdevs Connections' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

Google tokens are encrypted at rest on operator-controlled infrastructure. The encryption key is stored separately from encrypted-state backups. Application credentials and authorization files have restricted access. The mail service processes message bodies and attachment bytes in memory and does not persist them or log request bodies, credentials or codes.

Consumers may retain workflow state and imported records on their own protected storage. For example, Household retains used verification-message identifiers to prevent code reuse and keeps imported retailer receipts; the reporting application retains its imported reports. The public information site contains no account data, application credentials, analytics, forms or application cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-request metadata to serve these pages.

Retention and withdrawal

Authorization tokens are retained while an account remains connected. Account owners can revoke Connections access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings, or ask the operator to disconnect the account. Disconnecting revokes Google authorization and removes the active local token file after revocation succeeds. The operator can remove application grants independently and delete consumer records and retained backups. Copies in backups remain until those backups expire or are deleted.

Contact

Use the user-support email shown for Pepdevs Connections on Google's consent screen to request disconnection, deletion or information about retained data.
