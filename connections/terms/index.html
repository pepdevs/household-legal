Pepdevs Connections terms of use

Effective 7 October 2026

Private use

Pepdevs Connections is available only to its operator, approved account owners and approved applications. Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to manage. No public mailbox service is offered.

Authorization

Account owners grant Gmail read-only access on Google's pages and may withdraw that consent through their Google Account. The operator assigns separate service grants to approved applications. Those grants do not authorize uses beyond the owner's agreed workflow.

Availability and source records

Access depends on Google, the private network and the connected applications. Messages and imports may be delayed or unavailable when authorization expires or a provider changes. Original provider records remain authoritative.

Security and contact

The operator protects credentials, tokens and infrastructure. Account owners should report unexpected access through the user-support email shown on Google's consent screen and revoke authorization when needed.
