Household privacy notice

Effective 8 September 2026

Scope

Household is a private, self-hosted, single-user application. It is used only by its operator to manage the operator's own household finances. It is not offered to the public.

Data processed

Household processes account identifiers, balances, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions and statuses from accounts that the operator explicitly links. It can also process the operator's retailer ticket and receipt history. Authorization and collection metadata is retained so the operator can audit imports and prevent duplicate records.

Purpose

The data is used only to collect, organize, reconcile and review the operator's personal household finances. Household does not use the data for advertising, profiling other people, credit decisions or resale.

Sources and recipients

The operator authorizes data access through Enable Banking and the relevant bank. Retailer data comes from retailer accounts controlled by the operator. Data is not disclosed to other users. External providers process data only as needed to supply their own services.

Storage and security

Household stores collected data on private, operator-controlled infrastructure and its protected backups. Access credentials and active session state are restricted from the public web service. The public policy site contains no financial data, credentials, analytics, forms or application cookies.

Retention and control

Financial records and source evidence are retained while needed for the operator's personal records and backups. The operator can stop collection, revoke provider consent and delete local data. Enable Banking consents can also be reviewed and terminated through Enable Banking's consent-management service.

Contact

Use the data-protection contact address displayed for Household in the Enable Banking authorization and consent interface.
