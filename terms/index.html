Household terms of use

Effective 8 September 2026

Private use

Household is a private, single-user application. Only its operator may use it, and only with accounts and retailer identities that the operator owns or is authorized to manage. No service is offered to the public.

Account-information access

Household retrieves account identity, balances and transaction information after the operator gives explicit consent. It does not initiate payments or expose a payment function.

Authorization and withdrawal

The operator completes authentication and strong customer authentication on provider-hosted pages. The operator may stop collection or revoke consent at any time through the bank or Enable Banking.

Provider data

Banks, Enable Banking and retailers remain responsible for their services and source records. Household data can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable when a provider changes or interrupts its service. Provider records remain authoritative when they conflict with a local copy.

Security

The operator must protect application keys, provider credentials and the private infrastructure that runs Household. Suspected exposure requires session revocation and credential rotation.

Contact

Use the contact address displayed for Household in the Enable Banking authorization and consent interface.
